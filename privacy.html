Privacy Policy

Placeholder content for this demo site. The final policy is supplied by the business.

What we collect

When you contact Allsparks Electrical by phone or email we hold the details you give us, such as your name, contact information and the address of the job.

How we use it

Your details are used to quote, schedule and complete electrical work, to invoice for that work, and to keep the records required for electrical certification.

What we do not do

We do not sell your information or share it with third parties except where needed to complete your job or where the law requires it.

Your rights

Under the Privacy Act 2020 you can ask to see the personal information we hold about you and ask us to correct it. Contact us on 027 467 7464 to do so.
